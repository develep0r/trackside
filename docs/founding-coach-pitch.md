**Trackside watches for you.**

## What your clients do (60 seconds a day)

Your client gets a clean, private app on their phone. Every day they log weight, measurements, a progress photo, energy, sleep, and whether they trained — it takes under a minute, and the streak keeps them coming back. Their photos and numbers are private: visible only to them and to you, nothing social, nothing public.

## What you get (one dashboard, all clients)

Open your console on any laptop or phone and see your entire roster at once: who logged today, everyone's weight trend, week-over-week change, and — the part that pays for itself — an orange flag on anyone who's gone 3+ days without checking in. That flag is your daily to-do list. Message those two people before breakfast and you've done more retention work than most gyms do in a month.

When it's time for weekly feedback, Trackside drafts it for you from the client's actual week — their numbers, their notes, their goal — and you edit and send. Feedback that used to take 20 minutes per client takes two. Every client also sees your coach page: your photo, your philosophy, your credentials. Every invite you send is your brand, not ours.

## The math

A coach with 25 clients loses 2–3 every month to silent drift. If Trackside helps you save even **one client every two months**, that's ₹24,000–48,000 a year recovered — against a tool that will cost about the price of one training session per month.
